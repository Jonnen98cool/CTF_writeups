To solve this challenge, you need to think outside the box a little bit. You don't get much in the challenge. There's an image and audio of "Inspector Gadget" provided, but why wasn't the challenge placed in the "Steganography" category? You aren't meant to do anything steganography-related, instead you are meant to figure out that you need to inspect where the files are stored in CTFd, because they are placed in special subdirectory to the default `/files` directory. Combining the subdirectories of the image and the audio source, we get the flag: `Th1nk1ng_Out51d3_Th3_Ch4ll3ng3_B0x`
